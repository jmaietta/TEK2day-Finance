About

TEK2day Finance, from TEK2day Holdings, is a market data, fundamentals, macro and alternative data platform for institutional and retail investors.

Coverage

Data
Market Data Stock prices, valuation ratios, comparison tables, watchlists and ticker pages.
Fundamentals Financial statements, estimates, SEC filings, short interest and CEO analytics.
Macro Market, rates, money supply, inflation, growth and labor data.

Sources include Yahoo Finance, SEC EDGAR, FRED and CEORater. TEK2day Finance is not providing investment advice.

Founder

TEK2day Holdings

Jon Maietta founded and leads TEK2day Holdings. Before TEK2day, Jon led Solera's global Corporate Development organization and previously covered Enterprise Software, FinTech and Tech-Enabled Services as an equity research analyst with Needham & Company.
