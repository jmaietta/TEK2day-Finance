Jon Maietta

Founder & CEO

Jon Maietta is TEK2day Holdings' founder & CEO. TEK2day owns and operates FinTech platforms, products and content properties, including TEK2day Finance, Kilby, CEORater, TEK2day Pulse and the TEK2day Newsletter.

Prior to TEK2day, Jon led Solera's (the leading InsurTech company) global Corporate Development organization. Prior to Solera, Jon covered Enterprise Software, FinTech and Tech-Enabled Services as an Equity Research Analyst with technology investment bank Needham & Company, LLC.
